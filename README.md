# STARHERD

You are the Shepherd Wisp, a comet-bright spirit drifting over a sleeping
valley. Lost star-sprites called Strays wander the dark while shadow Umbra
hunt them. Fly loops with your ribbon of light — when the ribbon crosses
itself the loop snaps shut in a burst of gold, and every Stray inside joins
your glowing flock. Herd through six nights, choose constellation omens
between them, strip the veils from Vorath the Eclipse Hound, and seal the
Hollow Moon so dawn can break over the village below.

## How to play

- Move: mouse, arrow keys, or WASD — the Wisp chases your target point.
- Draw a loop around Strays to capture them into your flock.
- Wide loops (big area) are the only way to capture an Umbra.
- Closed loops leave an echo halo that keeps herding for a few seconds.
- Every 5 followers widens your ribbon; a bigger flock herds faster.
- Watch your Glow (top right). Umbra hits and idle darkness drain it;
  captures refill it. Glow at zero ends the run.
- Between nights, pick 1 of 3 omen cards (keys 1/2/3 or click). Waiting
  6 seconds picks the first card for you.
- P or Esc pauses, M mutes, Enter or click starts and restarts.

**best-game-01** bounty. It runs from plain static files under `game/`
with no build step and no network loads: open `game/index.html` through
any static server (or `node serve.ts`) in desktop Chrome and play.
